About InterProFinland

Empowering International Professionals to Thrive in Finland

We are a support network dedicated to helping international professionals overcome barriers, validate their qualifications, and build meaningful careers in the Finnish labour market.

1,800+ Community Members
80+ Professions Represented
74 Nationalities
2021 Established

Since 2021, we've built a vibrant community of international professionals, fostering connections, facilitating qualification recognition, and creating pathways to meaningful employment across Finland.

Our Story

How InterProFinland Began

When our founder, Olivia Kumpula, moved to Finland, she spent the first few years trying to find her place in her new home country. Through her expat blog and YouTube channel, 'My Finland Story', she documented her life and experiences in Finland and shared tips for integrating into Finland's society and culture.

She also started an interactive club on Clubhouse called 'The Finns and Foreigners Club'. Through connections made in the club, she realized that almost all international professionals encounter similar challenges which hinder them from utilizing their skills and transitioning into the Finnish workforce.

Although a number of resources are available, many internationals still have trouble locating these resources and applying them to their personal situations. In 2021, Olivia assembled a team of professionals and International Professionals Finland ry was established.

Our Mission & Vision

Why We Exist & What We're Building

A clear purpose guiding our work and a vision for an inclusive Finland where international expertise enriches the entire workforce.

Mission

Why We Exist

Providing a support network and tailored guidance to help international professionals in Finland overcome barriers, utilize their skills, and build meaningful careers.

Through diverse programs and services, we assist international professionals qualified for a profession abroad to overcome obstacles hindering them from utilizing their skills in Finland. We provide customized support, mentorship, networking opportunities, and one-on-one guidance as they take career steps toward transitioning into the Finnish workforce.

Vision

What We're Building Toward

An inclusive Finland with a balanced workforce that thrives on both local and international expertise.

We envision a future where international professionals seamlessly integrate into Finnish society, contributing their unique skills while the Finnish economy benefits sustainably from the diverse global expertise living here.

Our Values

What Guides
Our Work

Five core principles that shape every decision we make and every program we create.

We are an organization built on principles, not products.

  1. 01

    Potentiality

    We believe that there is an inherent ability in every single one of us which can be nurtured and developed to the fullest capacity.

  2. 02

    Recognition

    We believe that all humans deserve to be seen in the totality of their existence, not just in the fragment of the present, but as an entity with a past, skills, and identity that should be recognised.

  3. 03

    Professionalism

    We believe that underutilization of professional skills and training amounts to brain waste, and that harnessing the skills and talents inherent in a society enriches its economy.

  4. 04

    Growth

    We believe that Growth is a natural occurrence which is vital to human existence and wellbeing, and that everyone deserves a chance to grow professionally, economically, and in other areas they need to grow in.

  5. 05

    Inclusion

    We believe that a society is at its best if all who live within it are carried along in all areas, with none left devoid of the feeling of belongingness.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Support

Our work is organized around four interconnected focus areas that address the most critical challenges international professionals face in Finland.

01

Bridging Qualification Gaps

Navigating the Finnish qualification recognition process is complex. We provide step-by-step guidance through OPH, Valvira, and other competent authorities, helping professionals validate their international credentials.

02

Building Professional Networks

Access to the right connections transforms career trajectories. Through mentorship matching, industry events, and community clubs, we create opportunities for meaningful professional relationships.

03

Understanding Finnish Work Culture

Success in Finland requires more than qualifications. We help professionals understand Finnish workplace expectations, communication styles, and cultural nuances that make the difference.

04

Creating Pathways to Employment

From labour market orientation to job search strategies, we support professionals in identifying opportunities that match their expertise and building careers that reflect their true potential.

Recognition & Impact

European Good Practice Recognition

VII Call for Good Practices · ENIIE Madrid 2023

Our flagship Degree AMPLIFY project was recognized as a Finalist in the VII Call for Good Practices by the European Network of Innovation for Inclusion (ENIIE) in Madrid 2023.

This recognition highlights our innovative approach to supporting international professionals through the foreign diploma recognition process. Degree AMPLIFY provides structured guidance through OPH, Valvira, and other competent authorities, with mentorship from professionals who have successfully navigated the process themselves.
