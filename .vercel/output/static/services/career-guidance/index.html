Strategic Advisory

How We Support Your Career Journey

Our structured career guidance covers the essential components required to transition from international credentials to meaningful Finnish employment.

01

Finnish-Standard CV & Application Tailoring

Finnish employers expect concise, transparent, and impact-driven CVs. We guide you in highlighting transferable international experience, language capabilities, and technical competencies according to Finnish hiring standards.

  • ✓ Concise 1–2 page reverse-chronological layout formatted for Finnish ATS systems
  • ✓ Framing international projects and quantifiable achievements clearly
  • ✓ Tailoring targeted cover letters addressing specific role requirements
02

Unlocking the Hidden Job Market (Piilotyöpaikat)

Many open positions in Finland are filled through professional networks, direct recruiter outreach, and proactive applications before ever being advertised on public job boards. We provide systematic strategies for direct outreach and industry mapping.

  • ✓ Identifying growing regional companies and decision-makers in your sector
  • ✓ Constructing professional outreach messages in English and Finnish
  • ✓ Optimizing your LinkedIn profile for Finnish recruiters and search algorithms
03

Workplace Culture & Labour Legislation (TES)

Understanding Finnish working norms—flat hierarchy, direct communication, punctuality, and high autonomy—is essential for passing trial periods and building long-term career growth in Finland.

  • ✓ Collective labour agreements (Työehtosopimus / TES) and realistic salary benchmarking using industry standards
  • ✓ Trial period (Koeaika) expectations, employee rights, and occupational health orientation
  • ✓ Communication nuances, autonomy, and constructive feedback in Finnish teams
Interview Preparation

Mastering the Finnish Recruitment Interview

Interviews in Finland have distinct cultural expectations. Understanding these nuances helps you present your experience with credibility and confidence.

01

Directness & Authenticity

Finnish interviewers value factual honesty over exaggeration. Clearly state what you know, acknowledge areas you are learning, and give concrete, measurable examples of your past work.

02

Company Preparation & Strategy

Demonstrate thoughtful research on the organization's current operations, industry position, and technological ecosystem. Ask strategic questions about team workflows.

03

Salary Expectation & Negotiation

In Finland, job candidates are frequently asked for a salary request (Palkkatoive). We assist you in benchmarking realistic ranges using public union statistics and collective agreements.

04

Language Proficiency & Commitment

Clearly articulate your current Finnish language learning plan, even for English-speaking workplaces, showing long-term dedication to team integration.

Career Guidance FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our career advisory consultations and support scope.

What should I bring to a CV and career advisory session?

Please bring your current CV, links to your LinkedIn or portfolio, examples of job descriptions you are targeting, and any questions regarding Finnish working life.

Do you help with finding internships or first jobs in Finland?

Yes, we assist international graduates and newly arrived professionals in identifying entry pathways, trainee programs, and municipal employment initiatives.

Are career guidance consultations conducted in English or Finnish?

Consultations can be held in English or Finnish based on your preference and comfort level.

Can you review my contract before I sign with an employer?

We can provide general orientation on standard Finnish contract elements (working hours, trial period clauses, TES references). For legal disputes, we refer to specialized trade unions.
