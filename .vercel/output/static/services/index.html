Guidance Framework

Understanding Your Qualification Pathway in Finland

The procedure for using foreign qualifications in Finland depends primarily on whether your profession is legally regulated by Finnish legislation.

Mandatory Official Decision

Regulated Professions (Säännellyt ammatit)

Professions where Finnish law specifies required educational qualifications or official licensing before you are legally permitted to practice.

Typical Professions:

Healthcare professionals (doctors, nurses, dentists via Valvira), teachers & educators (via OPH), veterinarians, lawyers, maritime officers.

Next Step:

Requires formal application, transcript assessment, sworn translations, and often supplementary clinical/academic adaptation.

Comparative Academic Statement

Non-Regulated Professions (Muut ammatit)

Professions where Finnish employers independently assess your suitability and qualifications during the recruitment process without mandatory state licensing.

Typical Professions:

Software engineers, marketing specialists, business analysts, data scientists, project managers, graphic designers, HR specialists.

Next Step:

OPH comparability statement is optional but strongly recommended to clearly demonstrate educational level (Bachelor, Master) to Finnish employers.

Practical Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding eligibility, appointment scheduling, and advisory scope.

Are your advisory services and consultations free of charge?

Yes, all advisory consultations, CV reviews, qualification recognition guidance, and mentorship matching provided by InterProFinland are completely free for international professionals living in Finland.

How do I book an individual guidance consultation?

Consultations are organized by appointment on Mondays and Wednesdays. You can apply through our online application form or reach out directly to schedule a dedicated in-person or remote slot.

Do you submit OPH or Valvira applications on my behalf?

We guide you through the entire application preparation process, review your documents for completeness, and clarify requirements. However, the formal submission and state administrative fees remain the responsibility of the applicant.

Can I access your services if I reside outside the Satakunta region?

Yes! While our office is located in Pori, all individual guidance sessions, document reviews, webinars, and mentorship matching are available online for international professionals across all regions of Finland.
