Regulatory Authorities

Who Evaluates Your Credentials in Finland?

Different competent authorities handle specific fields. Each body has a defined scope — knowing which one governs your profession is the first step.

OPH
Higher Education & Teaching

Finnish National Agency for Education (Opetushallitus)

Evaluates foreign higher education qualifications for equivalence with Finnish university degrees (Bachelor, Master, Doctorate). Grants eligibility decisions for public sector posts, teachers, subject instructors, and early childhood educators.

Portal ↗
Valvira
Healthcare & Social Welfare

National Supervisory Authority for Welfare and Health

Grants official practice rights, professional registrations, and title protection for regulated healthcare and social welfare practitioners (doctors, nurses, dentists, pharmacists, psychologists, social workers).

Portal ↗
Ruokavirasto
Veterinary & Food Safety

Finnish Food Authority

Approves qualifications and issues official practice licenses for veterinarians, animal health practitioners, and food safety inspectors qualifying outside the EU/EEA.

Portal ↗
Traficom
Transport & Maritime

Finnish Transport and Communications Agency

Validates professional qualifications, maritime captain licenses, aviation pilot credentials, and specialized transport engineering endorsements.

Recognition FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions on foreign degree evaluation in Finland.

How long does the OPH degree comparability process typically take?

Processing times for OPH comparability decisions typically range from 2 to 4 months once all required certified documents have been received by the agency.

Do I need a decision if my profession is non-regulated (e.g. IT or Business)?

For private sector non-regulated roles, employers can hire based on skills without a state decision. However, an OPH statement officially confirms your academic level (e.g., Master’s level) and provides credibility.

What is the difference between an academic statement and professional recognition?

An academic statement compares degree level (e.g., your Bachelor equals a Finnish Kandidaatti). Professional recognition grants the legal right to practice a specific regulated profession in Finland.

How does InterProFinland assist with Valvira or OPH paperwork?

We review your documents for completeness, help clarify authority instructions, advise on certified translation requirements, and connect you with peers who completed the same process. Note that official decisions are issued solely by competent Finnish authorities.
