Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how International Professionals Finland ry (InterProFinland) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website to enhance your browsing experience and improve our services.

Last updated: January 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences (such as language, font size, and other display preferences) over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them whenever you return to the site or browse from one page to another.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Functionality: To enable core website features and ensure the site functions properly
  • Performance and Analytics: To understand how visitors use our website and identify areas for improvement
  • User Preferences: To remember your settings and preferences for a better experience
  • Security: To protect our website and detect malicious activity

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies without impacting the website's functionality.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
session_id Maintains user session state Session
csrf_token Security protection against cross-site request forgery Session

3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often and how users navigate the site. This data helps us improve the website's functionality and user experience. All information collected is aggregated and anonymous.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics - Distinguishes users 2 years
_gid Google Analytics - Distinguishes users 24 hours

Note: We currently use minimal analytics. As we develop our website, we may add privacy-respecting analytics tools such as Plausible or Fathom Analytics.

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as language preference or region) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
language_pref Stores language preference 1 year
cookie_consent Remembers your cookie consent preferences 1 year

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. Third parties may use cookies in the process of delivering content, including relevant advertisements, that may be of interest to you while on our website or other websites.

Third-party services we may use include:

  • Google Analytics: Web analytics service (if enabled)
  • Social Media Platforms: If you share content via social media buttons
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDN): For faster content delivery (e.g., Cloudflare)

These third parties have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies to understand how they use your data.

5. How to Control Cookies

You have several options to manage or disable cookies:

5.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or delete certain cookies. Note that if you disable essential cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly.

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

5.2 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our website footer.

5.3 Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of certain analytics services:

  • Google Analytics Opt-out: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

6. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies:

  • Web Beacons: Small graphic images (also known as "pixel tags") that track page views and email opens
  • Local Storage: Browser-based storage that allows websites to store data locally on your device
  • Session Storage: Temporary storage that is cleared when you close your browser

These technologies serve similar purposes to cookies and are subject to the same controls.

7. Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Currently, there is no industry standard for responding to DNT signals. We do not track users across third-party websites and therefore do not respond to DNT signals at this time. We respect your privacy and minimize data collection to what is necessary for website functionality and improvement.

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, our operations, or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.

9. More Information

For more information about how we handle your personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.
