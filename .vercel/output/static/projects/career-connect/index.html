Employment Program

Career Connect

Transition into the Finnish workforce with expert guidance and recruitment matchmaking.

Active 2022 – Present

What is Career Connect?

Career Connect is an employment program designed to enhance employability by assisting international professionals to acquire the necessary knowledge for transitioning into the Finnish workforce.

Through comprehensive workshops, personalized coaching, and direct recruitment matchmaking events, Career Connect bridges the gap between international talent and local employers in the Satakunta region.

Who is it for?

Career Connect is designed for international professionals in Satakunta who are currently unemployed, underemployed, or seeking to transition into roles that match their expertise and qualifications.

What You Get

  • Career Coaching: One-on-one sessions with career experts to develop your job search strategy
  • CV & Profile Optimization: Professional guidance on crafting Finnish-standard CVs and LinkedIn profiles
  • Workshops: Intensive hands-on training on job hunting, personal branding, and interview skills
  • Finnish Work Culture: Understanding the labour market, work culture, and professional expectations
  • Recruitment Matchmaking: Direct "Meet & Greet" events with local employers and recruiters
  • Networking Opportunities: Connect with companies actively hiring in Satakunta
