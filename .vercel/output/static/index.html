Why We Exist

Bridging Global Talent with Finnish Opportunities

International professionals often face barriers that prevent them from using their skills and qualifications in Finland. InterProFinland provides guidance, support and connections to help turn international expertise into meaningful opportunities.

We exist as an active network of individualized guidance, empowering professionals to build rewarding careers while enabling the Finnish economy to benefit sustainably from the global expertise living here.

Flagship Project

Degree AMPLIFY

Our flagship initiative empowering international professionals to navigate the official foreign diploma recognition process in Finland.

🏆 Madrid 2023 · European Finalist

Foreign Diploma & Qualification Recognition Support

Specialists who navigated the process guide new applicants step by step through competent Finnish authorities (OPH, Valvira). This initiative was recognized as an Innovative Good Practice Finalist by ENIIE and presented at the Madrid European Forum.

✓
OPH & Valvira Navigation: Guidance through official evaluation and licensing procedures for regulated and non-regulated professions.
✓
Specialist Mentors: 1-on-1 coaching from peers who already achieved credential recognition in Finland.
✓
European Good Practice: Recognized as an Innovative Good Practice Finalist by ENIIE and presented at the Madrid European Forum.
